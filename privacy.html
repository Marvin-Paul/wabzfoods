1. Introduction

At Wabz Foods, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled securely and responsibly. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when using our website and ordering services.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with Wabz Foods, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, phone number, and email address when you register an account or place an order.
  • Delivery Information: Your physical address or chosen pickup/delivery location (e.g., Ntinda, Kololo) to fulfill your orders.
  • Payment Information: Mobile Money (MoMo) numbers or masked payment card details. (Note: We use secure third-party payment gateways; we do not store full credit card numbers on our servers).
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, including cart contents and device type, to improve your experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • To process, confirm, and deliver your food orders accurately.
  • To send you important updates regarding your order status (e.g., "Out for Delivery").
  • To handle customer service inquiries, refunds, and feedback.
  • To improve our website's layout, menu offerings, and overall user experience.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Wabz Foods does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share necessary data with trusted third parties to facilitate your order:

  • Delivery Partners: Our riders receive your name, phone number, and delivery address solely for the purpose of bringing your food to you.
  • Payment Processors: Gateways like MTN Mobile Money or banking partners receive transaction details to securely process your payments.

5. Data Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your data. Your information is stored on secure, encrypted database servers (powered by Nhost). Access to customer data is tightly restricted to authorized Wabz Foods administrative personnel only.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website utilizes "Local Storage" (similar to cookies) to temporarily save your shopping cart, preferred delivery location, and session data. This ensures that if you accidentally close the browser, your delicious cart items are not lost. You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings.

7. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you. You may also request that we correct inaccurate data or delete your account and associated information entirely from our systems. To exercise these rights, please contact our support team.
